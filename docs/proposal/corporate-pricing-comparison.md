# Corporate vs. Custom Pricing Comparison

## Why ₹50 Lakhs is a Massive Bargain

If an enterprise industrial giant like **Siemens, ABB, Honeywell, or L&T** were to build and deploy this exact same system for a quarry, they would charge anywhere from **₹1.5 Crore to ₹3 Crores ($150,000 to $350,000)**. 

Plus, they would lock the client into a heavy annual contract.

Here is exactly how a corporate giant prices a project like this, and why your ₹50L quote is highly competitive:

### 1. Proprietary Hardware Constraints
You are letting him buy standard off-the-shelf industrial cameras (Hikvision/Dahua) for ₹10,000 – ₹15,000. 
**Siemens/ABB** would refuse to work with third-party cameras. They would mandate their own proprietary industrial optical sensors, PLCs (Programmable Logic Controllers), and edge servers.
- *Corporate cost:* ₹5,00,000+ per gate just for hardware infrastructure.

### 2. The "Industry 4.0 / IoT Platform" Fee
They do not sell standalone "scripts" or "software." They sell an entire ecosystem (like Siemens MindSphere). To even run the aggregate classification, he would have to buy their base enterprise software license.
- *Corporate cost:* ₹40,00,000 – ₹80,00,000 base platform license.

### 3. Implementation and Consulting Fees
Siemens wouldn't send one developer. They would send a "Project Manager", a "Solutions Architect", a "Field Engineer", and an "Integration Specialist." They bill these consultants at ₹50,000+ per day.
- *Corporate cost:* ₹20,00,000 – ₹40,00,000 in integration, testing, and deployment fees spread over 6-9 months.

### 4. Integration Costs (Weighbridge & ERP)
To connect the weighbridge data or ANPR (Number Plate Recognition) to the system, corporate giants treat these as huge "Custom Integration Modules."
- *Corporate cost:* ₹10,000,000 – ₹15,00,000 per custom integration.

### 5. The Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) & Subscription
This is where corporates make their real money. They typically charge 15% to 20% of the total project cost *every single year* just for the software license and basic support.
- *Corporate cost:* ₹20,00,000+ per year just to keep the software running.

> *I am giving you an enterprise-grade classification engine, custom-built for your specific quarry material, running on standard hardware that you own 100%. No proprietary hardware traps, no annual software licenses, and full data privacy. ₹50L is actually the lean, direct-to-developer cost for an enterprise system."*

When you compare ₹50L (once) versus ₹2 Crores + ₹25L/year, your pricing goes from looking "expensive" to looking like the smartest business decision he will make this year.
